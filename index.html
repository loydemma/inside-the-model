Inside the Model

Five things AI does, made visible.

01 · Attention

Every word weighs every other word.

Before a model answers, each word looks at all the others and decides which ones matter. Hold one and see what it pays attention to.

02 · Tokenization

AI does not read words. It reads pieces.

Type a sentence and cut the orange. Each slice is one token, the chunk of text a model actually sees.

03 · Temperature

One dial from predictable to wild.

Poke the jello, then turn the temperature. Low is steady and safe. High is loose and surprising.

04 · Generation

A reply grows one word at a time.

Press and hold to let the flower open. Each petal is one more word, picked from the candidates the model weighed.

05 · Agents

Big goals get broken into steps.

Tap the rocket to take it apart. An agent splits a goal into smaller steps and picks a tool for each one.
