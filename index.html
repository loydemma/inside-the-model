Inside the Model

Five surprising truths about AI, made visible.

01 · Tokens

How many r’s are in strawberry?

AI doesn’t see letters. It sees chunks, so counting letters is surprisingly hard for it.

Type any word or sentence, then swipe to slice.

For you: odd spelling, rhyming and letter-counting slips come from this.

For builders: tokens are what you pay for and what fills the context window; non-English text often costs more tokens.

02 · Next-word guessing

Does AI know facts?

It picks likely words, not true ones. That author doesn’t exist.

Press and hold to grow the answer, petal by petal.

For you: AI can sound confident while making things up, so check important facts.

For builders: this is why we ground models in real sources (retrieval/RAG) and ask them to cite.

03 · Temperature

Why does AI give a different answer every time?

A setting called temperature controls how much it gambles on less likely words.

Drag the slider, then poke the jello.

For you: ask again for fresh ideas, and expect answers to vary.

For builders: turn it down for consistent product behavior, up for brainstorming.

04 · Context window

Does AI remember everything you tell it?

AI only sees what fits in its window. Older messages fall out.

Tap to send another message.

For you: in long chats, early instructions get lost, so restate what matters or start fresh.

For builders: context windows are finite, so decide what stays (summaries, retrieval, pinned instructions).

05 · Agents

If each step works 95% of the time, how often does a 10-step task work?

Every extra step multiplies the risk. AI agents run many steps.

Drag the sliders, then press Launch.

60%

For you: AI agents stumble on long tasks, so check their work at key points.

For builders: shorten chains, add checks and retries, and keep a human in the loop for risky steps.
