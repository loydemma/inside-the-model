Inside the Model

Four surprising truths about AI, made visible.

01 · Tokens

How many letters does AI see in oranges?

Hover over the orange to grab your knife, then sliceSwipe across the orange to slice it

AI doesn’t see letters. It sees chunks, so counting letters is surprisingly hard for it.

You see 7 letters. AI sees 2 chunks.

Your turn: drag your knife across the orange to see how many tokens the word is.

The orange updates as you type. Then slice it, or press Enter to cut the next chunk.

AI reads in chunks called tokens, not letters. That’s why it can stumble on spelling or counting letters — and it’s also how AI usage is measured and priced.
